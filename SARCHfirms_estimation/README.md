Name of Quantlet: SARCHfirms_estimation

Published in: Spatial Effects in Local Productivity Volatility: A Non-Dynamic Spatial ARCH Analysis of Romanian Localities

Description: Main estimation of the paper. Fits the non-dynamic spatial ARCH and SARspARCH models of Otto, Schmid and Garthoff (2018) to the two way fixed effect residuals of locality labour productivity, year by year over the 16 annual cross sections, and also to the unfiltered variable so that the spatial autoregressive term in the mean can be seen before filtering.

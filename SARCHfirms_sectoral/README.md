Name of Quantlet: SARCHfirms_sectoral

Published in: Spatial Effects in Local Productivity Volatility: A Non-Dynamic Spatial ARCH Analysis of Romanian Localities

Description: Tests whether the spatial effect is in fact a sectoral effect. Neighbouring localities often share the same industrial structure, so a sectoral shock would show up as spatial clustering. The same Moran statistic is computed under a geographic neighbourhood, under a sectoral neighbourhood built from the CAEN division composition of each locality, and under a geographic neighbourhood after the shocks are orthogonalised on the principal components of that composition. Produces the first three rows of Table 2.

Keywords: sectoral composition, CAEN, principal components, orthogonalisation, Moran I, spatial versus sectoral clustering, Romania

Author: Codruta Mare, Mihai-Adrian Achim, Gabriela-Mihaela Muresan

Submitted: 23. Nov 2026, Mihai-Adrian Achim

Input: panel_uat.rds from SARCHfirms_data and the INS business register for the CAEN structure

Output: confuzie_sectoriala.csv
